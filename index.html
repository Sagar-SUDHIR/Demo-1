One sweet, three southern kitchens

Every summer, when the mango trees are heavy, families across the south turn the extra fruit into a roll that lasts the year. Each state has its own name, its own touch.

South Karnataka

ಮಾವಿನ ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ಸಿಹಿ ರೋಲ್

Pulp from sweet Badami and Raspuri mangoes, dried on cloth in the courtyard and finished with a pinch of jaggery.

Kerala

മാമ്പഴ റോൾ

The tang of Kerala's kadukka mango and a whisper of cardamom, spread over banana leaf and dried until glossy.

Andhra Pradesh

మామిడి తాండ్ర

Mamidi tandra is built up in thick, patient layers, each dried before the next goes on. Chewy, deep and sweet.

From the tree to your tea time

Four steps, no shortcuts. It takes about three days of sun to make one roll.

  1. Pick

    Fully ripe, sweet-sour mangoes, chosen at the farm gate.

  2. Pulp

    Peeled, pressed and strained. Nothing added except a little jaggery.

  3. Dry

    Spread thin on trays and dried in the sun, one layer at a time.

  4. Roll

    Cut, rolled by hand and packed fresh in sealed pouches.

Questions, answered

What is mango roll?

A chewy fruit leather made from sun-dried mango pulp. It's called mambalam in some homes, mamidi tandra in Andhra and aam papad in the north.

How long does it stay fresh?

Six months in the sealed pouch. After opening, keep it in an airtight box and eat within three weeks.

Is there added sugar or colour?

No artificial colour or preservatives. A little jaggery is added to balance the tang.

How long does delivery take?

2 to 3 days in South India, 4 to 6 days elsewhere in India.

Can I order for a gift or in bulk?

Yes. Add the 1 kg pack, or write to us for wedding and festival orders.
